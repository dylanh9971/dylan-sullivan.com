my take on design

Good design, like a good story, needs imagination

Which is hard to find when things start from a blank canvas, black and white. Even on the most colourful projects, design escapes through spacing breaks, labels that don’t match and stale interactions. Only with the right framing and a little imagination can you solve these issues.

Telling a story requires structure in some capacity but you don’t have to make it rigid, sometimes exaggerating key parts of it is what makes the story shine, similar to websites and product design, you have to exaggerate things. It’s the bar, set long ago, if it doesn’t stand out, you’ll move on, as you should.

The right amount of information, in the right order, at the right time.

What’s on this page is the surface. Behind each project there’s research, a fair bit of redrawing, and a hundred small decisions that never make it into a screenshot.
